Elevating Digital Commerce for Artisanal Brands

A comprehensive redesign of the e-commerce experience, focusing on high-end typography, minimal layouts, and immersive product storytelling.

Client

Lumina Atelier

Role

Lead Product Designer

Timeline

Q3 2023 - 12 Weeks

Deliverables

UX/UI, Design System

The Challenge

Lumina Atelier, a boutique ceramics brand, struggled with a digital presence that felt disconnected from the tactile, premium quality of their physical products. The existing site suffered from cluttered navigation, inconsistent branding, and a checkout process that hindered conversion rates.

The goal was to create a digital experience that mirrors the feeling of walking into a high-end gallery space—minimalist, deliberate, and entirely focused on the craftsmanship of the objects.

Strategic Approach

We adopted an 'editorial-first' approach. By treating product pages less like standard e-commerce listings and more like articles in a high-end design magazine, we could tell a richer story.

  • check Implemented a fluid grid system to ensure perfect breathing room for imagery across all devices.
  • check Reduced the cognitive load in the checkout flow by removing unnecessary fields and introducing a single-page, stepped process.
  • check Developed a monochromatic core palette punctuated by deep, organic accent colors to reflect the ceramic glazes.
